## What I Offer

- **Written Technical Report:** A detailed, data-driven analysis covering specifications verification, real-world performance, thermal behavior, power consumption, software compatibility, and practical usability.
- **Video Review (Optional):** A companion video walkthrough demonstrating setup, benchmarks, real-world use cases, and a clear explanation of methodology.
- **Open-Sourced Methodology:** All test procedures, tools, and raw data will be made publicly available for reproducibility.

## Suggested Evaluation Areas

When possible, I recommend covering the following dimensions:

1. **Linux Compatibility** – Kernel module support, out-of-the-box device recognition, driver availability, and any proprietary firmware requirements.
2. **Performance** – Real-world benchmarks across representative workloads, including synthetic and application-based tests.
3. **Power & Thermal Behavior** – Idle, load, and sustained-power measurements; thermal throttling behavior under prolonged stress.
4. **Interfaces & Connectivity** – USB, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, display outputs, audio, and any proprietary connectors.
5. **Practical Demonstration** – Real-world use cases that matter to end users, not just benchmark numbers.

## Transparency Commitment

I am committed to balanced, honest reporting. If you provide a sample, loan unit, or any form of compensation, I will clearly disclose this in all published materials. My evaluations will not be influenced by the provision of hardware or sponsorship.
