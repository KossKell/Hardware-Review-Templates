# Video Review Outline

## 1. Intro and Disclosure

- Introduce the product being reviewed.
- State clearly that this is an independent review with no manufacturer sponsorship or paid arrangement.
- Mention how the review unit was obtained (purchased, sample, etc.).

## 2. Unboxing / Board Tour

- Show the packaging and included accessories.
- Tour the board: point out key components (SoC, RAM, connectors, LEDs).
- Note build quality and labeling.

## 3. Setup

- Describe how the board was powered and connected.
- Detail the OS/image used.
- Show initial boot and system identification.

## 4. Methodology

- Explain the testing methodology used.
- List tools and measurement equipment.
- Describe how results were recorded.

## 5. Demos

- Show practical demonstrations of key features.
- Include both successes and failures where relevant.
- Be transparent about limitations observed during demos.

## 6. Measured Results

- Present quantitative results (benchmarks, measurements, throughput).
- Use charts or tables where appropriate.
- Include raw data references.

## 7. Limitations

- Be honest about what could not be tested or what conditions may affect results.
- Note any equipment or software constraints.

## 8. Verdict

- Provide a balanced, honest summary.
- State who might benefit from this product and who might not.
- Avoid hyperbole; stick to evidence-based conclusions.
